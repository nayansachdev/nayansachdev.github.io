02 — About Me

Designing at the intersection of people, products and engineering.

I am studying Product Design Engineering at Loughborough University, where I am developing skills in product development, engineering analysis, prototyping and computer-aided design.

My projects explore how research and practical engineering can be combined to create products that address real-world problems.

I enjoy taking ideas from initial concepts through to CAD models and physical prototypes, while considering usability and the needs of the user.
